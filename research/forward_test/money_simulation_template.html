1 contract per trade: real entry/exit option premiums straight from forward_test_log.csv, x100 for the standard contract size, exactly 1 contract every time — a single lot on GS (entry $36.13 → $3,612.50 committed) carries ~7x the dollar risk of a single lot on CAG (entry $0.40 → $40), purely because of the underlying's own share price, unrelated to trade quality.

Fixed $ risk per trade: contracts sized so (entry − stop) × 100 × contracts ≈ the risk budget shown above, for every trade. Some names (flagged above) can't be sized down to the budget even at 1 contract — shown honestly, not forced.

What neither view is: a sizing recommendation for live capital. Commissions/slippage aren't modeled. Open positions show capital committed only, never a guessed unrealized P&L.
